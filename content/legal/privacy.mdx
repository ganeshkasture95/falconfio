This policy explains what personal data Falconfio collects through this website, why, and the choices you have. Falconfio is a technical brand of **GK TechMatrix** ("we", "us"), which is responsible for your data.

## What we collect

**When you contact us.** If you use the contact form, an agent request, the estimator or the scorecard and then send us your details, we receive what you enter: usually your name, email, company and a description of your project. We use it only to reply to you and to discuss your project.

**When you email us.** We receive your email address and whatever you include in your message.

**When you browse.** Our hosting provider records basic technical information, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to keep the site secure and working. We do not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

**On your device.** The site stores one small setting in your browser's local storage to remember whether you've hidden the on-page guide (Falco). It never leaves your device and you can clear it at any time.

The free tools (Architecture Scorecard and Project Estimator) run in your browser. Your answers are not sent to us unless you choose to share them through the contact form.

## Why we use it

- To reply to your enquiry and prepare for a conversation (our legitimate interest, or steps you ask for before a contract).
- To deliver services we agree with you (contract).
- To keep the website secure and running (legitimate interest).
- To meet legal obligations, such as tax and accounting records.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it to train AI models.

## Who we share it with

Only with service providers who help us run the site and reply to you, under contracts that protect your data:

- **Vercel**, which hosts the website.
- **Resend**, which delivers form submissions to our inbox by email.
- Our email provider, which stores our correspondence with you.

We may also share data if the law requires it. Some providers may process data outside your country; where they do, we rely on appropriate safeguards.

## How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as we are in conversation with you. If no project follows, we delete them within 24 months. Project records are kept for as long as the law requires.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and the EU and UK GDPR, you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, object to or restrict its use, or withdraw consent. To make a request, email [hello@falconfio.com](mailto:hello@falconfio.com). We reply within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data-protection authority.

## Children

This website is meant for businesses and is not aimed at children. We do not knowingly collect children's data.

## Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
